About

I direct AI like a DP directs light.

I'm Seif Eldein Amgad Abd Elazem, an AI commercial director. Commercial video used to mean a crew, a permit, and a six-figure budget. Today it means knowing exactly which model, which prompt structure, and which grade turns a generated clip into something a brand can put its name on. Over the past year, that's the gap I've been working in — creative direction fluent in both film language and the AI pipeline behind it.
